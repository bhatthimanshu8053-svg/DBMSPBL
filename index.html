Food Storage

Record how prepared food is stored and see precautionary alerts.

Alerts are precautions only, based on the simple rules below and on the data you enter. They do not guarantee that food is safe or unsafe. Always follow your canteen's food-safety procedures.

Rules used for alerts
  • Room temperature for more than 2 hours → warning
  • Hot holding below 60 °C → warning
  • Refrigerated above 5 °C → warning
  • Refrigerated for more than 72 hours (about 3 days) → warning
  • Frozen above -18 °C → warning
  • Temperature not entered where a rule needs it → information message

These are commonly used general food-safety guidelines. Check your local regulations for exact limits.
